# krosval — naming history (T01, resolved)

**Outcome: the project is named `krosval`** (binary `krosval`, optional alias `kv`), settled 2026-08-05. Repo: https://github.com/amit-t/krosval. This file preserves the trail; the live record is T01 in `tickets.md`.

## Why krosval

It reads as **cross-validation** — the statistical practice of splitting data into many folds that cross-check each other to produce one validated estimate *with a confidence measure*. That is exactly what the council does: many independent agents, cross-reviewed, synthesized into one answer with a confidence readout. Amit chose it by veto over the runner-up `kroval` (cleaner to type, but no meaning hook).

## How we got here

1. **Working title `parallax`** carried the project through charting while naming stayed open.
2. **Early grilling rounds** rejected Sanskrit/council-literal names (sabha, panchayat, mantrana, darbar), physics/astronomy deep cuts (plenum, syzygy, orrery, althing), legible council words (conclave, quorum, chorus, roundtable), and mechanism coinages (parallax, consilience, murmuration, condorcet).
3. **A six-model Devin brainstorm** (Fable 5, Opus 4.8, Sonnet 5, GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, SWE-1.7) generated ~90 candidates and recommended **`talvane`** (runner-up `zynth`). Rejected — invented mashups whose meaning didn't land.
4. **Meaning-first pivot:** real words in the "many pieces → one picture" family. `mosaic` clicked but collides hard with Databricks' **Mosaic AI** (its own CLI), and the whole tile family — `tessera`/`tesserae`, `terrazzo`, `stipple` — was occupied, several by rival multi-agent coding tools. Structural dead end: legible single words here are already taken.
5. **Amit proposed two coined finalists — `kroval` and `krosval`.** Picked **`krosval`** for the cross-validation reading.
